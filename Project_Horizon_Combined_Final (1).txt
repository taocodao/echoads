    2. MARKET DISRUPTION & OPPORTUNITY OF CDN TRANSITION 

  The CDN industry is undergoing its most fundamental transition since the shift from broadcast to IP. Traditional HTTP-based CDNs 
  cannot support real-time sports interaction, synchronized multiview, live in-play betting, or sub-second commerce. Media over QUIC 
  (MoQ), now under IETF standardization, achieves sub-second latency at broadcast scale. The world’s first real-time MoQ CDN was 
  demonstrated  at  CES  2026,  confirming  commercial  readiness.³  This  timing  creates  a  unique  window  for  TowerCos  and  TelCo  to 
  reposition their stranded edge capacity as the foundational footprint of a next-generation MoQ CDN, leapfrogging legacy models by 
  going directly to a MoQ-based, edge-native platform. 

  Free ad-supported streaming (FAST) now reaches 131 million US viewers, 54% of all connected TV households, growing at 43% year 
  over year.¹ More than 70% of net new streaming subscriptions since 2023 chose ad-supported plans.¹ Sports is the fastest-growing, 
  highest-value segment, and EchoStar’s content relationships and audience equity position it to lead. That infrastructure is ready now.
